Full-Spectrum Capabilities

Turnkey Construction &
Bespoke Interior Services

From foundation stone laying to architectural 3D styling, modular kitchen fabrication, and luxury paint finishing—explore our complete suite of residential and commercial services.

01

New Construction & Civil Work

Turnkey end-to-end structural construction for independent houses, luxury villas, builder floors, and commercial buildings. We handle everything from soil testing and excavation to RCC framing, column casting, brick masonry, plastering, and waterproofing.

Structural Engineering: Earthquake-resistant RCC frame with Tata Tiscon 550D TMT steel and UltraTech cement.
Precision Masonry: High-density red clay bricks / AAC lightweight blocks with polymer-modified plaster.
Approvals & Vetting: Soil test certification, structural stability certificates, and municipal compliance.
02

Property Renovation & Remodeling

Upgrade, modernize, and structurally optimize your existing residential or commercial property. We carry out wall alterations, room expansions, elevation facelifts, bathroom overhauls, and complete plumbing/electrical retrofits.

Layout Reconfiguration: Non-load bearing wall removals, open-kitchen conversions, and room resizing.
Waterproofing & Repairs: Deep structural crack injection, Dr. Fixit chemical waterproofing, and damp isolation.
Facade Modernization: Modern exterior cladding (HPL, Shera boards, toughened glass railings, texture paint).
03

Architectural Planning & 3D Visualization

Every great structure starts with immaculate planning. Our architects provide Vastu-compliant 2D space planning, structural drawings, photorealistic 3D elevations, and virtual walkthroughs before physical construction begins.

Vastu-Compliant 2D Floor Plans: Optimized light, ventilation, zoning, and circulation pathways.
Hyper-Realistic 3D Renders: Visualizing textures, lighting fixtures, furniture scale, and facade colors in 4K resolution.
Detailed Working Drawings: Electrical conduit layouts, plumbing schematics, beam cross-sections, and joinery details.
04

Interior Design (Home & Office Interiors)

Create refined, luxurious living and working spaces. Our turnkey interior division handles full home decor, master bedrooms, living lounges, acoustic home theatres, corporate boardrooms, and open workstations with premium materials and ergonomic finishes.

Custom Woodwork & Wardrobes: BWP IS:710 Marine ply carcasses, soft-close sliding wardrobes, and fluted panel accents.
Commercial Fitouts: Soundproof glass partitions, ergonomic height-adjustable desks, breakout zones, and HVAC routing.
Soft Furnishings & Wall Decor: Accent wallpapers, charcoal louvers, brass inlays, and customized upholstery.
05

Modular Kitchens & False Ceilings

The heart of your home engineered with German precision. We build high-gloss acrylic, PU painted, and textured laminate modular kitchens complete with Blum/Hettich tandem boxes, quartz counters, and custom Gypsum false ceilings.

100% Boiling Waterproof (BWP) Core: Marine ply tested to withstand steam, humidity, and daily heavy usage.
German Hardware & Organizers: Soft-close lift-ups, pantry pullouts, corner magic carousels, and cutlery trays.
Designer False Ceilings: Saint-Gobain Gyproc moisture-resistant boards with shadow profiles and magnetic track lights.
06

Specialized Works (Electrical, Plumbing, Painting & Flooring)

The invisible backbone and tactile finishing that defines longevity. We execute fire-retardant electrical circuitry, pressure-tested CPVC plumbing, Italian marble diamond polishing, and luxury Royale emulsion wall finishes.

Concealed Electrical MEP: FRLS Polycab/Havells copper wiring, Schneider modular switches, and MCB protection.
Plumbing & Sanitary: Astral CPVC/UPVC noise-dampening lines, Jaquar/Kohler diverters, and wall-hung WC systems.
Flooring & Luxury Paint: Italian Statuario marble, 4x2 vitrified tiles, epoxy joint grouting, and Asian Paints Royale Luxury Emulsion.
