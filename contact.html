Frequently Asked Questions

Is the initial site inspection and consultation free of charge?

Yes, 100% free! Our senior civil engineer or architect visits your plot or property, reviews spatial dimensions, discusses your vision, and provides a preliminary feasibility and budget estimate without any cost or obligation.

How does NEXA BHARAT guarantee transparent pricing with no hidden costs?

We provide a line-by-line Bill of Quantities (BOQ) detailing the exact brands of cement, steel, plywood, fixtures, and paint. Once the agreement is signed, the rates are fixed and there are zero unapproved price variations throughout the project lifecycle.

What is your project payment schedule?

Payments are divided into milestone stages (e.g. Excavation & Foundation, RCC Framing, Brickwork & Plastering, MEP Concealed Works, Interior Finishes, Handover). You only release the payment for a stage after your personal inspection and engineer signoff.

What warranty does NEXA BHARAT offer on construction and interior work?

We provide a 10-year structural warranty on new civil construction and a 10-year warranty on modular interior woodwork and German fittings, backed by an official warranty certificate issued upon project handover.
